A few useful answers

Before the
opening scene.

Not sure where to begin? That's a perfectly good place to start.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Start with the task or business problem you want to improve. We can work through the options together.

Can you work with tools we already use?

That is a useful place to start. Share the tools and workflow you have today so we can account for them.

How do you decide what to build first?

We look at where customers or work get stuck, then agree on a practical first improvement and a clear scope.

Do you guarantee a specific business result?

No. Outcomes depend on many factors. Work should have clear goals, honest reporting and room to improve based on evidence.
