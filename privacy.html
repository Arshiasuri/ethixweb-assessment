Privacy

Your enquiry details are for responding to your enquiry.

This draft describes information collected through this website. Ethixweb should review and approve it before collecting production enquiries.

Information you submit

The enquiry form collects the details you provide: name, email, optional company and budget, phone number, service interest, and project description. It also records your consent to be contacted.

How it is used

These details are intended to help Ethixweb review and respond to your enquiry. If configured, they are stored in the connected Supabase database and available to authorized administrators. No advertising trackers are included in this project.

Retention and requests

The actual retention period, deletion process and production service providers must be confirmed by Ethixweb before launch. For privacy questions, contact info@ethixweb.com.

Before launch

This draft is not legal advice. Have the organization responsible for the website confirm this notice, applicable privacy requirements, retention periods and contact details before collecting enquiries.
